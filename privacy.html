Privacy Policy.

This policy covers theklipstudio.com and explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. KLIP is operated by Klip Studio LLC and Klip Media LLC, 2145 Davie Blvd, Suite 204, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33312.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you book a room or a session, request a consultation, or submit the Visibility Check form, you provide details such as your name, email address, phone number, business name and the website address you asked us to analyze.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, payment card numbers by email or through this website's forms.

How we use it

To schedule and deliver what you booked, to answer your enquiry, to send the confirmations and reminders attached to a booking, and to improve the website.

The Visibility Check analyzes the public web address you enter and returns a score based on what is publicly available at that address. If you ask for the results by email, we use your address to send them and to follow up about KLIP services.

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

Bookings for studio rooms, photography and tours are handled by SimplyBook.me, which processes your booking details and any deposit under its own terms and privacy policy.

Consultation calls are scheduled through Calendly, under its own terms and privacy policy.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. If analytics are enabled, Google Analytics may set cookies to measure how the site is used.

Each of these companies handles the data it receives under its own policy. We recommend reading them if that matters to you.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies of its own. Embedded booking and scheduling tools, and analytics if enabled, may set their own cookies when you use them. Your browser settings control whether you accept them.

How long we keep it

Booking and client records are kept for as long as we need them to run the business and meet tax and legal obligations. Enquiries that do not become work are kept for a reasonable period and then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@theklipstudio.com or call 954-541-3708 and tell us what you want done.

You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in the message.

Photography and footage

Work produced for a client belongs to that engagement and is not published by KLIP without permission. If we would like to show your project publicly, we ask first.

Imagery on this site that is not KLIP's own production is licensed stock, used under its license.

Children

This website is intended for business customers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Questions go to info@theklipstudio.com or 954-541-3708.
