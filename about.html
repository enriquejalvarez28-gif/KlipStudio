Questions

Before You Book..

What is KLIP?

KLIP is a content, production and digital marketing company with KLIP Studio and KLIP Media under one brand. We help businesses, creators and professionals create content, build visibility and grow.

Can I book the studio without a technician?

Yes. Selected studio sessions are available self-service. If you prefer help with setup, production or recording, technician and full-service options are also available.

What can I create at KLIP Studio?

Podcasts, interviews, reels, UGC, personal-brand content, online courses, photography, product content, live-selling sessions and other branded content.

Can I record an online course at KLIP?

Yes. Courses, lessons, masterclasses and training content can be recorded in different KLIP rooms depending on the look and format you want.

Do you provide cameras, lighting and audio?

Equipment depends on the room and booking type. KLIP Studio offers professional cameras, lighting and audio setups, and the booking page shows what is included with each service.

Can I bring guests or my own crew?

Yes, subject to the capacity and rules of the room you book. The booking client is responsible for their guests and crew while they are on the premises.

Can I book a photographer?

Yes. You can book a photography session and choose an available photographer, or book/rent the photography space depending on the service.

Do you offer editing after the session?

Yes. Podcast editing, reel editing and other post-production services can be added depending on the project.

Do you offer marketing services too?

Yes. KLIP Media provides services including SEO, GEO/AI search visibility, paid advertising, social media, branding, websites, content strategy and AI solutions.

Can KLIP come to my business or event?

Yes. On-location production is available for businesses, events, interviews, campaigns and other content needs.

How do I book?

Studio services are booked through the embedded KLIP booking experience. Marketing and strategy consultations are scheduled through the embedded consultation calendar.

Where is KLIP located?

KLIP Studio is at 2145 Davie Blvd, Suite 204, Fort Lauderdale, FL, serving South Florida including Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach.
