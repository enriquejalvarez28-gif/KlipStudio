# Sales Strategy

1. **Research** each business (public sources only). Verify identity; label every fact VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN.
2. **Qualify:** verified identity, relevant industry, a real evidence-backed opportunity, a matching Klip service. No opportunity → skip.
3. **One angle per prospect:** the strongest single opportunity (video, local SEO, GEO, website conversion, ads creative, branding, social). Don't pitch everything.
4. **Cold email (Harvey):** 80–130 words, one observation, one opportunity, one question. Approval required for every email.
5. **Reply → GoHighLevel:** Harvey stops instantly and hands off (note, history, task, opportunity). Salesperson continues in the same thread.
6. **Offer ladder:** 15-min call → Content Day ($699) or a scoped Media retainer.
7. **Ramp:** 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 emails per day by week from a separate domain.
