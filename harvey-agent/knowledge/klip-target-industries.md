# Target Industries & Markets

| Tier | Industries | Why Klip fits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Med spas / aesthetics, cosmetic dentists, plastic surgeons, dermatologists, chiropractors | Visual, high-ticket, trust-driven; patients research the provider on video/social first |
| 2 | Law firms, physical therapy / sports medicine, orthodontists, hair restoration, optometry / ophthalmology | Authority content, FAQ video libraries, local SEO |
| 3 | Wellness / longevity, real estate & mortgage | Personal-brand content, green-screen listing videos |

**Pilot:** 25 med spas + 25 cosmetic dentists.
**Cities first:** Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Weston, Davie, Plantation, Hollywood, Southwest Ranches; then Miami, Boca Raton.
**Exclude:** national chains/franchises and DSOs (marketing is decided at HQ).

Regulated-industry rules: no patient info, no outcome claims (medical); bar ad rules (legal); NMLS + Equal Housing (mortgage); Fair Housing (real estate).
