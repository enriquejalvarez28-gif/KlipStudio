# Klip Brand Voice

Full rules: ../skills/klip_brand_voice.md and ../skills/klip_copywriting.md (used by the agent).

- A local business owner writing to another one. Direct, warm, professional. Short sentences.
- Bilingual: reply in Spanish when they write in Spanish; native, not translated.
- One observation, one opportunity, one ask. No feature dumps, no prices in cold email.
- Never: "unlock your potential", "next level", "revolutionize", "skyrocket", "transform your digital presence", "I hope this finds you well", em dashes, exclamation marks.
- Creativity is welcome when it's specific to the business. Templates are a floor, not a script.
- Visual brand (for any graphics): Montserrat; #1D1D1C, #EBEBEB, #E40E20 (red, accents only), #5D5D5C.
