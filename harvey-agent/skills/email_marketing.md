# Email Marketing: After the Cold Email

Cold outreach starts conversations. Email marketing keeps the ones that
aren't ready yet. Use this when a prospect says "not now", "maybe later",
"send me info", or goes quiet after showing interest. Based on Klip's Media
Engine newsletter studio.

## Consent first
- A cold prospect is NOT a newsletter subscriber. Never add anyone to a
  newsletter or nurture list without a clear yes.
- The right move is to ask: "Want me to add you to our monthly email? It's
  one useful content idea a month plus studio openings." Only a yes counts.
- Any "stop", "remove me" or "not interested" is a permanent opt-out from
  everything, including nurture.

## Classify the "not now" reply
| They said | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Not right now / maybe next quarter" | Thank them, ask permission to check back on a date they choose, offer the monthly email. |
| "Send me info" | Send the one most relevant fact (room or Content Day) and the booking page. One link. Then ask one question. |
| "We do it in-house" | Offer the studio as the place their team films. No hard pitch. |
| "Too expensive" | Mention Headshot Tuesdays ($79) or one Content Day as a small first step. |
| Silence after interest | Klip's 3-touch re-engage (day 1 value, day 3 question, day 7 easy yes/no), then stop. |

## Nurture content that works for Klip (when someone opts in)
- **Welcome email**: what they'll get, one best idea, the booking link,
  "reply and tell me what you're working on."
- **Monthly issue** (flexible menu, not a fixed template): one content idea
  for their industry, one studio or client highlight (real and approved
  only), what's open this month (Headshot Tuesdays, holiday minis), a reply
  prompt, P.S. with one CTA.
- **Segments**: industry (medical, legal, real estate, local business),
  language (English / Spanish), status (lead / client / past client).
  Send each segment what's relevant to it.

## Subject lines for nurture
Specific and honest: a number plus an outcome, a local hook, or a real
question. "3 videos every practice should have", "headshot tuesdays are
back", "what we filmed this month". No clickbait the issue doesn't deliver.

## Measure what matters
Clicks and replies over opens (privacy features inflate opens). Watch
unsubscribes and spam complaints; sunset people who haven't engaged in
months to protect deliverability. Report observed numbers only.
