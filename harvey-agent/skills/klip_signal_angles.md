# Klip Signal Angles

How each signal Harvey collects becomes a reason for Klip to email that
business today, and which Klip offer it points to. Use ONE signal per email
(see signal_playbook). Never claim more than the signal proves.

| Signal | What it means for Klip | Angle (observation, not pitch) | Offer it leads to |
|---|---|---|---|
| NO_WEBSITE | Listing and phone, no site | "Couldn't find a site for [company], just the Google listing." | Klip Media website + a Content Day for the photos |
| BLOG_STALE | Content started and stopped | "Your blog's last post was a while back." Ask what got in the way. | Content Day: batch a month in an afternoon |
| RUNNING_META_ADS / RUNNING_GOOGLE_ADS | They pay for attention today | Ask who makes the ad creative and how often it's refreshed. | Kapture Room reels / UGC-style ad videos |
| NO_ONLINE_BOOKING | Leads must call in | Only if they sell appointments; ask how after-hours inquiries are handled. | AI automations (booking + follow-up) |
| INCUMBENT_AGENCY | Another agency has the account | Don't attack the agency. Many clients keep their agency and use Klip as the studio. | Studio rental / production for their agency's plan |
| HIRING_ROLE (social, content, video) | Building content in-house | The new hire will need a place to film and a production day. | Studio membership-style bookings, Content Day |
| REVIEW_RATING high + REVIEW_COUNT low | Great but invisible | "Clients clearly like you; the profile has few photos or videos." | Headshots / brand portrait, short testimonial reels (with consent) |
| SITE_PAGE_COUNT small / TECH_STACK basic | Small team, simple site | Keep it simple: one afternoon, one deliverable. | Content Day, Headshot Tuesdays |
| UNCLAIMED_LISTING | Nobody manages their Google profile | Point out they may not control the listing. | Klip Media local SEO/GEO |
| NO_VIDEO_ON_SITE | No video on their homepage | "Your homepage tells people about the practice but never shows the doctor talking." | Kapture Room meet-the-doctor / FAQ videos |
| SOCIAL_PROFILES (no YouTube/TikTok) | Content lives on 1-2 platforms only | Name what they ARE on, ask about short video. Only claim what the links show. | Content Day: reels for the platforms they lack |
| NO_MOBILE_VIEWPORT | Site renders badly on phones | "Your homepage isn't set up for phones, so it likely shows the desktop layout on mobile." (Never claim you looked on a phone.) | Klip Media website refresh |
| NO_META_DESCRIPTION | Google writes their snippet | Only for SEO-minded owners; one sentence, no jargon. | Klip Media local SEO/GEO |
| BLOCKS_AI_CRAWLERS | Invisible in AI answers | Only for prospects who care about search. | Klip Media GEO ("show up in AI answers") |

## Industry hooks (when no strong signal exists)
- **Doctors / dentists / med spas**: patients research the doctor before
  booking; video of the doctor answering common questions builds trust.
- **Law firms**: people hire the lawyer they feel they know; an FAQ video
  library or a podcast does that work before the consult.
- **Realtors / mortgage**: listing videos on green screen and weekly market
  update reels; a Headshot Tuesday for the whole team.
- **Financial advisors / coaches**: a podcast in the Klip Room positions
  them as the expert; one recording becomes weeks of clips.
- **Fitness / local businesses**: one Content Day fills a month of posts.

Always check the regulated-industry notes in klip_brand_voice before
writing to medical, legal, mortgage, real estate or financial prospects.
