## Who buys and why
- **Medical practices, med spas, dentists**: need trust-building video
  (meet-the-doctor, FAQs, procedure explainers) without filming in the
  clinic. Compliance: no patient info, no outcome guarantees.
- **Law firms**: authority content and a podcast or video FAQ library. Bar
  advertising rules apply to anything we make for them.
- **Realtors and mortgage pros**: listing videos on green screen, market
  updates, headshots, personal-brand reels. Fair Housing; MLOs need NMLS IDs.
- **Financial advisors, coaches, founders**: a podcast or a monthly batch of
  direct-to-camera reels for their personal brand.
- **Local businesses**: a Content Day to fill a month of social in one afternoon.

## Buying triggers
- Posting on social has stalled or never started
- Website or Google profile has no video, or photos are years old
- Running ads with weak creative
- Hiring a social media manager or content creator (we can cover the
  production side, or be the alternative)
- Launching a podcast, course or personal brand
- New office, new partner, rebrand or new location

## Main objections (handle with the objection skill)
- Price: lead with the Content Day; one afternoon covers a month.
- Already have an agency: we can be their studio; many clients keep both.
- No time: batching is the point; one session, a month of content.
- Camera-shy: teleprompter, a technician in the room, and we direct you.
