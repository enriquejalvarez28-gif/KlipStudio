PHOTOGRAPHER CALENDARS — NO NEW TOOL
------------------------------------
   Each photographer becomes a Provider in SimplyBook under the
   Photography category. Then:
     - SimplyBook → Manage → Service Providers → add the person and
       set their working hours.
     - Give them a login at Access Level 2, which lets them manage
       only their own schedule and see only their own bookings.
     - Turn on the Calendar Sync custom feature and have each person
       connect their OWN Google account. Busy events in their Google
       calendar then block those hours on the KLIP booking page.
   Rules that matter: one Google account per provider, never two
   providers on the same calendar; events must be marked Busy, not
   Free; only Google and Outlook web sync — iCloud does not.
   Last, paste each person's SimplyBook provider id into
   /admin → Photographers, and "Book With <Name>" opens that
   person's own availability.
